{ HUMANISE }


by Avani


one of the first projects i picked up at Plum was Humanise, an editorial platform created for the purpose of showcasing frames of human experience through the lens of corporate life.


this volume is on exits, ebbs, and interludes.


while illustrating for it, I had spent a long time looking for a specific ‘moment’ to expand on, before I realized there isn’t one. i concluded that an interlude doesn’t name an event, that it’s a gap between one act and the next.

what i chose to land on to stylistically represent breaks is that act of daydreaming.

i chose to use animals to carry the writers’ words. almost everything about how we leave, and how we're let go, already exists somewhere in the wild :
creatures have been outgrowing their homes, sensing the tide turn, the molt, the migration - these are all recognizable to us in many contexts in our lives, and the resemblance is ours to notice or not.

shift: "Reading the Signals: A Framework for Understanding Why People Leave"

Saaquib Dawoodani talks about signs of employee departure, and the reasons as to what pushes employees to leave a space.

study:"The Postmortem of a Layoff"

Abha Ahad writes a detailed and exploratory account of the aftermath of a layoff and its impact on employees, their lives, and the ripple effect it creates.

emotion: "Someone's Paying for Your Career Break (It's Not You)"

Lavanya Mohan describes the phantom pain of taking a career break, and the weight of having to justify monetary sacrifices.

foresight: "Try Not to Burn Bridges."

Sameera Khan describes the importance of maintaining relationships and the potential consequences of damaging them.
