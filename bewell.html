{ BANGALORE WELLNESS MAP }



Avani, Asawari, Ganapathi Ramanathan, and team


The 'wellness' that we talk about is a broad term that encompasses physical, mental, and emotional well-being.

The Bangalore Wellness Map was made in an effort to be a comprehensive guide to wellness resources in the city, and general ways to keep healthy for those visiting or living in the city.

We hoped it would be a useful place to go to find a nearby park or lake to circuit, a fun alternative fitness class to try, or to plan days around to build healthy habits.

This is an ongoing project that we aim to keep updating for new cities. We started with Bangalore, as we are locals and headquartered there, and Mumbai, where we have friends, but keep looking for the other metro cities maps soon!

EXTRAS ✧˚₊‧

WHY A WELLNESS MAP?

Plum is a company in the business of care. The idea was to make wellness feel more accessible and less intimidating. We wanted to move beyond the conventional understanding of health as something limited to gyms, diets, and medical check-ups, and explore the many ways people can take care of themselves in their everyday lives.

WHAT COUNTS AS WELLNESS?

We approached wellness as a combination of physical, mental, and emotional well-being, along with subcategories for ease of use. This meant including everything from running trails, communities, fitness studios to healthy food options, healthcare services, and spaces for mental well-being. The idea was to make room for different interests, needs, and ways of living.

HOW IS THE DATA COLLECTED?

We brought together wellness resources from across the city, organizing them by category and location to make discovery easier. Alongside established businesses and services, we also included communities, events, and online resources. The map is an evolving collection rather than an exhaustive directory.

Our data comes from Google Maps, OpenStreetMaps, community forums and word of mouth.

HOW DOES THE PLAN MY DAY FEATURE WORK?

Beyond finding individual places, we wanted to help people imagine what a day centered around their well-being could look like. The Plan My Day feature brings together curated activities around a chosen wellness theme, making it easier to turn inspiration into an actual plan.

Our aim was to create a usable service that can keep people engaged with the map by providing a whole day planner as a main feature.

WHY BENGALURU FIRST, AND WHY MUMBAI NEXT?

We started with Bengaluru because it was home. Being locals gave us a starting point to explore the city's wellness ecosystem, understand its communities, and discover the many ways people engage with health and well-being. Mumbai was the next step in taking this idea beyond a single city, exploring a new urban landscape and its own distinct wellness culture. We hope to keep expanding the map to other cities, building a growing resource that makes discovering and accessing wellness opportunities easier, wherever you are.

CAN I SUGGEST A PLACE?

Absolutely! Wellness is constantly evolving, and there will always be places, communities, and experiences we haven't discovered yet. The map is intended to keep growing, and users can suggest places that they think deserve to be included.

If you have information about a wellness resource that's missing from the map, or if you'd like to update existing information, please contact us through the website.
