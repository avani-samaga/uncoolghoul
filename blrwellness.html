{ BANGALORE WELLNESS MAP }



Avani, Asawari, Ganapathi Ramanathan, and team


The 'wellness' that we talk about is a broad term that encompasses physical, mental, and emotional well-being.

The Bangalore Wellness Map was made in an effort to be a comprehensive guide to wellness resources in the city, and general ways to keep healthy for those visiting or living in the city.

We hoped it would be a useful place to go to find a nearby park or lake to circuit, a fun alternative fitness class to try, or to plan days around to build healthy habits.

This is an ongoing project that we aim to keep updating for new cities. We started with Bangalore, as we are locals and headquarted there, but keep looking for the other metro cities maps soon!
